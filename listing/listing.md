# Adobe Express listing: what to paste

Package: dist.zip (npm run build && npm run package)

Add-on name (25): SnapDub Clips
Summary (50): Add your SnapDub short clips to any design

Full description (1000):
Bring the vertical clips SnapDub made from your videos straight into Adobe Express.

SnapDub turns long videos, streams and podcasts into short vertical clips with captions. With this add-on your finished clips are one click away while you design. Sign in with your SnapDub account, pick a video, and click a clip to add it to your page, or drag it right where you want it.

What you can do
• See your SnapDub videos and their clips, best first
• Add a clip to your page with one click, or drag and drop it
• See each clip's length and score before you add it
• Start a new video on snapdub.ai and come back to its clips

Getting started
Open the add-on and press Sign in with SnapDub. No account yet? Create a free one from the sign-in window. Make clips on snapdub.ai and they show up here.

Please only add clips from videos you have the rights to use.

Monetization: Free and paid plans (the add-on is free; clips are made with SnapDub credits, free account included).

AI usage: The add-on does not generate anything inside Adobe Express. It imports video clips the person already made in their SnapDub account. SnapDub uses AI to pick the moments and to write the captions from the speech; it does not generate new video or voices for these clips.
